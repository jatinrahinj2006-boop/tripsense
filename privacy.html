Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we handle your data.

What Data We Collect

TripSense is designed with privacy in mind. We collect only minimal data stored locally on your device using localStorage:

  • Favorite cities — Cities you've marked as favorites
  • Recent searches — Your last 4 searched cities for quick access
  • Dark mode preference — Your theme choice (light or dark)

We do not collect any personal data such as your name, email, phone number, or location history. No account registration is required.

Cookies

TripSense does not set any cookies directly. The only data stored is via your browser's localStorage API, which remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide functionality:

  • OpenWeatherMap — Weather data, air quality, and UV index (via Cloudflare proxy)
  • Groq AI — AI-powered travel assistance (via Cloudflare proxy)
  • Google Maps — Location embedding for visualizing cities
  • Unsplash — High-quality destination images
  • Cloudflare — CDN and API proxy services
  • Google Fonts — Typography (Outfit, Manrope, Material Symbols)
  • Lucide — Icon library

These services may have their own privacy policies. We use Cloudflare Workers to proxy API requests, keeping your actual location data more secure.

No User Accounts

TripSense does not require user registration or accounts. You can use all features anonymously without providing any personal information.

Location Data

If you choose to use the "Use my location" feature, your coordinates are used only to fetch weather data for that location. This data is not stored or logged anywhere.

Data Security

Since all data remains on your device (browser localStorage), data security is handled by your browser's built-in security mechanisms. We recommend keeping your browser updated for the best security.

Clearing Your Data

You can clear all stored data at any time by:

  • Clicking the Clear Chat button in the AI chatbot
  • Removing individual favorites using the trash icon
  • Clearing browser data/site data for thetripsense.in in your browser settings
